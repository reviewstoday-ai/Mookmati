Why choose a Mookmati bedsheet

Our bedsheets are made the slow way. In Rajasthan, artisans carve patterns into wooden blocks, dip them in dye and press them onto cotton by hand, one impression at a time. The result is a bedsheet with real character, where every flower carries the touch of the person who printed it.

  • Pure cotton: soft, breathable and cool, ideal for Indian summers and comfortable all year.
  • Large size: 108 x 108 inches, which fits king and super king beds with room to tuck in.
  • Gentle choices: natural dyes wherever the craft allows, and no silk. Our work is guided by ahimsa, the path of non-violence taught by Acharya Shri Vidyasagar Ji Maharaj.
  • Fair price: from ₹950, with free shipping across India.

Bedsheet questions

What size are Mookmati bedsheets?

Our bedsheets are 108 x 108 inches (king, king plus or super king). They fit king and super king beds, and drape generously over queen beds.

What are the bedsheets made of?

They are made of 100% pure cotton, which is soft, breathable and cool on warm nights.

What is hand block printing?

Artisans press carved wooden blocks dipped in dye onto the cloth, one impression at a time. Small variations in the print are the mark of handwork, not a flaw.

How much do the bedsheets cost?

Our bedsheets are priced from ₹950, with free shipping anywhere in India. International shipping is available at an extra charge.

How should I wash a block-printed cotton bedsheet?

Wash separately in cold water with a mild detergent for the first few washes, and dry in shade to keep the colours fresh.
