Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Tap "Order on WhatsApp" on any product. We confirm price, stock and delivery time in the chat, then share payment details.

How can I pay?

We share payment options on WhatsApp when we confirm your order.

How long does delivery take?

We tell you the expected delivery date on WhatsApp before you pay. See our Shipping Policy for details.

Can I return or exchange a product?

Yes. See our Returns and Exchanges policy.

Why does my product look slightly different from the photo?

Handmade products vary a little from piece to piece. Colours may also look different on different screens. These variations are part of the craft and are not defects.
