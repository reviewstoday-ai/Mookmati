Our story

Named after the silent earth

Mookmati takes its name from Mookmati, the epic by Acharya Vidyasagar Ji. At its heart is humble clay, shaped through patience into something useful and beautiful.

We choose what we sell in the same spirit. Every piece is made by hand. Every choice starts with one question: does this cause harm?

Read our story

Ahimsa is not only what we avoid. It is how we choose to make things.

Non-violence, as taught by Acharya Vidyasagar Ji, guides our brand. It shapes the materials we pick, the artisans we work with, and the pace at which we work.
