The Art Behind the Fabric: Waxed Handblock Printing 🌾

Unlike mass-produced machine prints, these table runners are printed entirely by hand using the traditional waxed handblock printing technique.

  1. Intricate Carving: Skilled artisans meticulously carve breathtaking Rajasthani motifs into wooden blocks.
  2. Wax Resist Printing: Hot wax is applied to the blocks and pressed firmly onto the pure cotton fabric to create a beautiful, crisp resist pattern.
  3. Natural Dyeing: The fabric is dyed, and once the wax is washed away, a stunning, intricate design is revealed.

Because every single piece is handcrafted, no two runners are exactly identical. You aren't just buying table linen, you are bringing a unique piece of wearable art into your home.
